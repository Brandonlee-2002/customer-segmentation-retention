Executive summary

This report will identify meaningful household segments, estimate the probability that each household returns during the holdout period, and recommend a non-equal $1,000,000 marketing allocation. The final recommendation must distinguish predicted return probability from incremental marketing opportunity.

Data and customer-level feature construction

The unit of analysis is one household. The feature period is the first 75% of the observed transaction window, and the holdout period is the remaining 25%. The outcome retained equals 1 when the household makes at least one purchase during the holdout period.

Limitations and next steps

This analysis estimates association, not causal marketing lift. The budget recommendation should be validated with randomized treatment and control groups, tracking incremental purchases, revenue, margin, retention, and cost per incremental retained household. The final analysis should also document assumptions about contact costs, discount costs, and customer-level profitability.
